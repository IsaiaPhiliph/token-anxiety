# Code of conduct

Token Anxiety is a small project, and we want everyone who takes part in it to feel welcome, whatever their background, identity or experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good faith.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Respect people's privacy: do not share anyone's personal data, tokens or screenshots without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling and deliberate disruption.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block people from the project, temporarily or for good.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/IsaiaPhiliph). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
